# Shot composition

Use one focal point per shot. Rank attention by size, contrast, color and position. Record the default with `direction.focalPoint`: `left-third`, `center`, or `right-third`.

Think in three depth layers: background/ambient, midground/support, foreground/hero.

Use one major camera intent per beat: push/pull, pan, follow, or parallax. Avoid stacking large push + pan + rotation without a story reason.

For vertical 9:16 keep critical text roughly inside top 12–14%, bottom 18–20%, sides 6%. For landscape use about 5% margins. Restack instead of blindly cropping.

For routes, leave visual room in the direction of travel and use `cameraAnchorY` plus a separate camera path when needed.
